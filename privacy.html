BabySounds for Sleep Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026 · Versión en español

BabySounds for Sleep (the “app”) is developed by Isidro de la Rosa. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on servers, share or sell personal data. It requires no account or sign-in, contains no ads, analytics or tracking tools, and works offline.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your preferences (language, color theme, volume, timer and playback settings) on your own device so it can remember them next time. This information never leaves the device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Permissions

The app only uses what it needs to play audio in the background. It does not access the microphone, camera, location or contacts.

Ratings

If you choose “Rate the app”, your store’s rating system opens (Apple App Store or Google Play), governed by Apple’s or Google’s privacy policy respectively.

Children

The app is intended for parents and caregivers. It does not collect data from any user, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page with its update date.
